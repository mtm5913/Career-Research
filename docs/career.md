## What this changed in the portfolio

- **Lead with the research process.** Show questions, methods, observations, and limits before suggesting solutions.
- **Make individual contributions visible.** My eight diary-study opportunities, my two Spotify interviews, and my Olive review decisions are different kinds of evidence.
- **Show inspectable work.** Link code, learning records, and checks, while accurately disclosing AI assistance.
- **Treat improvements as hypotheses.** A proposal is not a shipped feature, and a check is not proof against every failure.

## Two directions, one provisional choice

Research emphasizes understanding people and interpreting evidence. Product engineering and FDE work emphasize building and delivering systems as well as understanding the problem. The research side is my strongest interest; learning the implementation side helps me ask better questions and collaborate.

## What I’m developing next

Usability testing, evidence-to-design traceability, and evaluation of AI behavior against expected results. My next milestone is a real-user study of Olive with a documented improvement and retest.
