## 1. Purpose and research question

I am an HCI bachelor's student expecting to graduate in **May 2028**, with full-time summer availability. My strongest interest is UX research, with an additional interest in design and how AI is used in practice.

**Research question:** Where do understanding people, research, and AI meet in everyday work—and what should I develop to pursue that direction?

The research compares three original employer postings. It includes FDE as a reference, an internship, and an experienced product-engineering role. These are three examples to learn from, rather than a complete picture of the job market.

## 3. What the comparison shows

### UX research using AI

The center of the work is planning studies, interpreting evidence, and communicating findings. AI supports the research process. This direction aligns most closely with my interest in understanding people's behavior.

[Explore UX research and the Deltek reference →](./roles/ux-research)

### Forward deployed engineering

The center of the work is understanding a customer's workflow and delivering a working technical solution. The Provectus reference adds programming, integration, evaluation, and deployment expectations.

[Explore FDE and the Provectus reference →](./roles/fde)

### AI product engineering

The center of the work is turning user needs into working products across interfaces and services. The Anthropic reference connects design judgment to implementation, agent reliability, and iteration with external users.

[Explore AI product engineering and the Anthropic reference →](./roles/product-engineering)

**Provisional choice:** UX research involving AI-enabled products or AI-assisted research workflows. Technical learning can help me understand system limits and collaborate with engineers. Deltek is a UX research alternative; using AI in research does not make it retrieval engineering or model evaluation.
