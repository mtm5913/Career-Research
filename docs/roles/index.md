# Compare three career directions

My question is not just “Which title sounds interesting?” It is “What work would I do regularly, and what evidence would show I can begin doing it?”

| Dimension | UX research using AI | Forward deployed engineering | AI product engineering |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employer reference | Deltek | Provectus | Anthropic |
| Center of the work | Understand people and communicate findings | Understand customer workflows and deliver systems | Own user-facing products across interfaces and services |
| AI's role | Support research processes | Help build and evaluate AI-enabled solutions | Power the product and agent behavior |
| Typical outputs | Studies, synthesis, research repository | Working integrations, retrieval, evaluation, deployment | Features, full-stack systems, reliability improvements |
| Entry signal in this sample | Degree enrollment and practiced research methods | STEM degree, AI projects, programming fundamentals | Substantial production ownership and external-user iteration |
| My relevant starting point | Spotify interviews and annotations | Olive AI-assisted building and checking | Diary-study findings translated into my feature proposals |

## Where the families overlap

All three involve understanding problems and communicating decisions. FDE and product engineering both involve coding and delivery; FDE emphasizes customer-specific workflows, while the Anthropic example emphasizes product surfaces and an agent runtime. These are interpretations of these postings, not universal definitions.

## Where the assignment categories fit

Provectus fits the listed FDE family; Anthropic fits AI product engineering. Deltek is a UX research alternative using AI. It is **not** classified as retrieval engineering or AI-model evaluation merely because AI supports the research workflow. Earlier exploration included AI evaluation, but these are the three selected records.

## My provisional direction

UX research is the strongest fit with my interests in behavior, interviewing, synthesis, and evidence-informed design. Technical learning remains useful for understanding AI limitations and collaborating with engineers. This is a provisional direction, not a claim of qualification for all three positions.
